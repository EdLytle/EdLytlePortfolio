This was my first game that I published to phones. You made the little purple guy run around, collect cards, and make the evil monsters get stuck in bushes. Obviously, something I regularly ate around this time must have consistently had something funny in it.










Image Credit: Ed Lytle

This was my first attempt at making a web browser game. You control the triangle and try to avoid being pushed to the left side of the screen by the squares. Not exactly a technological advancement, but it did lay the groundwork for my favorite project to date.











Image Credit: Ed Lytle

This project marks shifting from making browser games to entertain my friends to making brain puzzles with the goal of mitigating mental blocks, this one specifically being made for math anxiety. You move the alien around, collect multiples of a certain number, and try not to get pushed off the board by the meteors. Here is my website, where you can learn more.









Image Credit: Ed Lytle

This is my current project, which I was called on by a specialist to make. It is another brain puzzle with the goal of helping symptoms of Dyslexia. You are given words, and you can only pick the letters that are actually part of that word. It's still being worked on, so you can read up on what the specialist wanted me to keep in mind in the meantime.
