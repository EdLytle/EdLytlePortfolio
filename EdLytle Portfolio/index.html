Who Is This Guy?

Ed Lytle was born on October 11, 2003. He is a student at North Central College and an aspiring video game developer. While all aspects of digital design, such as websites, graphics, and more, are interesting to him, games are especially so due to their ability to open new doors for people through their heavy focus on interactivity. Though, all art and design is interesting and valuable to him. Starting on this career path by making simple web browser games in high school, he has since found a niche in making small puzzle games that help mitigate learning disabilities and mental blocks in people.
